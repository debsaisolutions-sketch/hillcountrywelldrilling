Why Is My Well Producing Sandy or Sediment-Filled Water?

Homeowners in the Hill Country often enjoy the benefits of having a private well, but one common issue they may face is sandy or sediment-filled water. Understanding the causes and possible solutions can help you maintain a clean and efficient water supply for your home.

Common Causes of Sediment in Well Water

Several factors can lead to sediment or sand in your well water. Identifying the root cause is the first step in addressing the issue.

1. Well Pump Location

If your pump is placed too close to the bottom of the well, it might draw in sand and sediment. This is particularly common in areas where the well depth fluctuates with water levels.

2. Well Casing Issues

A damaged or improperly installed well casing can allow sand and sediment to enter the water supply. Regular inspections can help identify casing problems before they worsen.

3. Naturally Occurring Sediment

Some areas in the Hill Country naturally have higher sediment levels in the groundwater. In these cases, additional filtration may be necessary to ensure clean water.

DIY Solutions for Sandy Water

Once you've identified the cause of the sandy water, there are several DIY strategies you can try before calling in a professional.

  • Adjust the Well Pump: If possible, raising the pump higher above the well floor can reduce sediment intake.
  • Install a Sediment Filter: Sediment filters can be an effective solution for homes experiencing minor sediment problems. These filters are relatively easy to install and maintain.
  • Regular Maintenance: Ensure your well system is regularly inspected and maintained to catch potential problems early.

When to Call a Professional Well Driller

If DIY solutions do not resolve the issue, or if you suspect a more serious problem, it’s time to call in a professional. A local well drilling expert can offer a comprehensive assessment and provide the necessary repairs or adjustments.

Whether it's adjusting the pump placement or repairing the well casing, professional assistance ensures that the job is done correctly and safely.
